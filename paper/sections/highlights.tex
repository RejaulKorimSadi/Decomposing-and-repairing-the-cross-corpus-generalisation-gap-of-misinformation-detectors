\item Ten audited corpora, seven detectors and a zero-shot LLM, tested across corpora.
\item About 70\% of the cross-corpus F1 gap is lost ranking; the rest is the threshold.
\item EM label-shift correction worsens the threshold because calibration breaks.
\item Flagging the source base rate fixes most of the threshold loss without labels.
\item Domain-generalisation methods do not beat pooling: corpus and label are entangled.
